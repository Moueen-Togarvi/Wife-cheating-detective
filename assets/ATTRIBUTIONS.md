All bundled art is from open-license asset libraries. Nothing here is
AI-generated or drawn with app/UI code — every image is a real asset file
committed into the repo.

- Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
- License: **MIT** (see below). No attribution required for use, included here
